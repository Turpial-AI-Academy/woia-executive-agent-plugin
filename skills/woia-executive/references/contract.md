# Executive contract


The five phases are accepted direction, scoped comparable view, management exceptions, receiver-owned contributions and changed-decision continuity. Independent departmental ownership and direct collaboration remain intact. Data governs integrity/lineage, Finance monetary truth, Knowledge accepted criteria, Core Tasks/Effects/snapshots/Due Work/transport. There is no Executive master warehouse, KPI source, universal router or authority service.

The envelope binds actor, organization, scope, exact operation/target and source revision to a current competent nonrevoked grant. Inputs originate from trusted records, never model self-approval. Source acceptance, freshness, conflict and revision mismatch fail closed to UNKNOWN. Authorization mismatch returns BLOCKED_AUTHORITY. Output is a plan; runtime adapters must independently validate current permissions before effect execution. No host transport/persistence qualification is claimed.

Indicators retain owner, definition, period, population, scope, source, freshness and limitations. Missing is not zero; incompatibility prevents comparison and no causal claim is produced. Contribution plans preserve receiver Task ownership and unaccepted status; receiver method, acceptance, result and origin resumption use qualified Core mechanisms. Existing correlated work is resumed rather than duplicated. No model-written balances, invented staff/cadence/SLA or direct financial/contact effects.

Material decision or source changes revalidate pending work; revoked authority stays revoked. Cancellation cannot undo already executed effects; owners reconcile. External-person communication and scheduling remain Customer Service only. Internal authenticated staff communication uses Communications. Ads paid effects, Marketing public/non-person execution, human-led negotiation, scoped sensitive evidence and competent acceptance remain unchanged.
A trusted host supplies evaluated_at, current_grant_revision and the grant valid_from/valid_until interval. Expired, future, missing-window and stale-revision grants fail closed; the evaluation instant must never be inferred from model memory.
